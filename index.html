About me

A lion in the lobby. A fan on game day.

I’m FerrousLion — a gamer, content creator, streamer, and lifelong sports fan.

I believe gaming is about more than winning. It’s about the experiences, the unexpected moments, the laughs, and the people you meet along the way.

You’ll usually find me dropping into Fortnite, exploring expansive game worlds, taking on a new adventure, or enjoying a little friendly competition. Sometimes my son, FerrousCat, joins me for Fortnite Duos. Other times, it’s just me, the game, and whoever happens to be hanging out in chat.

When I’m away from the controller, I’m following Auburn and Miami Dolphins football, along with Manchester United and Inter Miami soccer. I enjoy talking sports just as much as playing games.

Whether you’re here for the streams, the games, the sports conversations, or simply the community, you’re welcome.
